SPECIALIZED RODENT CONTAMINATION CLEANUP

Rodent Contamination Requires More Than Regular Cleaning

Rodent activity can leave behind droppings, urine, nesting materials, contaminated dust and debris in attics, crawlspaces and other areas that may be difficult to access and safely clean.

Our team focuses on careful removal of contaminated materials, HEPA filtration when appropriate, detailed cleaning and professional decontamination to help return affected areas to a cleaner and more manageable condition.

OUR APPROACH

Rodent Contamination Cleanup May Include

Rodent Droppings & Debris Cleanup

Careful removal of rodent droppings, nesting materials and contaminated debris from affected areas.

Detailed Cleaning

Detailed cleaning of surfaces and areas affected by rodent activity and contamination.

HEPA Filtration

HEPA filtration equipment may be used when appropriate to help manage airborne particles during the cleanup.

Professional Decontamination

Cleaning and decontamination of surfaces affected by rodent contamination using professional products and procedures appropriate to the conditions encountered.

Odor Treatment

Additional odor-treatment options may be available for areas affected by persistent odors associated with rodent contamination.

Property Recovery

Our goal is to help return affected areas to a cleaner, safer and more manageable condition after contamination cleanup.
