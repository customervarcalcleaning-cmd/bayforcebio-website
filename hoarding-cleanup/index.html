SPECIALIZED PROPERTY CLEANUP

When a Property Needs More Than Regular Cleaning

Hoarding conditions can involve excessive accumulation of belongings, heavy debris, persistent odors, animal waste, contaminated surfaces and areas that have not been accessible for routine cleaning.

Our team approaches these properties systematically, focusing on cleanup, decontamination and property recovery while treating the situation with discretion and respect.

OUR APPROACH

Hoarding Cleanup May Include

Debris & Clutter Cleanup

Systematic cleanup of accumulated debris and materials to regain access to affected areas.

Deep Cleaning

Detailed cleaning of surfaces and areas that may not have been accessible during regular maintenance.

HEPA Filtration

HEPA filtration equipment may be used when appropriate to help manage airborne particles during the cleanup.

Professional Decontamination

Cleaning and decontamination of affected surfaces using professional products and procedures appropriate to the conditions encountered.

Odor Treatment

Additional odor-treatment options are available for properties affected by persistent or severe odors.

Property Recovery

Our goal is to help return affected areas to a cleaner, safer and more manageable condition.
